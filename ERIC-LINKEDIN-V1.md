## 20. Evidence tests

Before publishing the rebuild:

1. **Mobile preview test.** Open LinkedIn on mobile, view profile as a visitor. Headline + About first sentence + Featured row must all land cleanly on one screen.
2. **Cold reader test.** DM the rebuild to 3 people outside your network. Ask: `would you click through to the site?` Target 3 of 3 yes.
3. **SSI score check** (linkedin.com/sales/ssi) two weeks after rebuild. Target 70+.
4. **Search discoverability test.** Have someone log out, search `Chief AI Officer Montana` + `AI Operating Architect` + `Fractional Chief AI Officer` + `Level9 OS`. You should appear in the first 3 results on all four.
5. **Post-engagement baseline.** Note first-post reach + comments. Target: Week-4 reach at 2x Week-1.
