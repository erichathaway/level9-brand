Product Spec . Signal Engine for Outbound + Content + Relationships

The signal engine for the operator who lives on LinkedIn.

LinkUpOS turns LinkedIn from a chore into an operating surface. Inbound signal monitoring, outbound personalization, content scheduling, and relationship telemetry all run from one calibrated voice profile. 30 orchestrated workflows. One unified Daily Briefing. Multi-LLM routing per task.

01
Intro . What it is
A signal engine, not a posting tool.

LinkUpOS watches your network for relevance, turns inbound signal into ranked opportunities, drafts outbound in your voice, and posts content on schedule. It runs as 30 orchestrated workflows on n8n with a Next.js dashboard on Vercel and a Supabase data plane.

  • Voice-calibrated. One profile, three motions: comment, post, message.
  • Tier-gated personalization. Sonnet 4.6 daily, Opus 4.7 for high-value comments.
  • Daily Briefing. Six morning emails replaced by one prioritized digest.
  • Append-only telemetry. Every signal, draft, and post logged.
02
Build Scope . What ships
Six engines. One operating console.
  • Signal Engine. Inbound monitor + relevance scoring across feed, comments, DMs.
  • Outbound Engine. Apollo prospects, voice-calibrated drafts, tier-gated send.
  • Content Engine. POV docs to posts, narrative arcs, scheduled queue.
  • Relationship Engine. CRM-grade telemetry. Strategy calls. Funding events.
  • Intel Engine. Weekly roll-ups. Company pages. Topic metadata.
  • Brand Engine. Theme tagging. Voice drift detection. Library.
03

04
Impact . Why it matters
Hours back. Reach up. Voice intact.

The operator who used to spend two hours scrolling, drafting, and second-guessing now spends fifteen minutes triaging a single Briefing. The voice is theirs because the model was trained on theirs. The output is auditable because every step writes a row.

Architecture . The Build, End to End

How it runs.

Three planes. Six tiers. One audited path from external signal to logged action. Postgres + RLS at the data plane. n8n on Synology NAS at the function plane. Vercel + Next.js at the surface plane. Multi-LLM routing per task, with model choice tied to task tier.
