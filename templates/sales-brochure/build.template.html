GOVERNANCE LAYER

In 2026 nobody trusts AI that runs without guardrails. The governance layer makes the rest of the stack defensible to your CISO, your board, and your auditor. Every action logged. Every output verified. Every system replaceable mid-flight.

01 . Measurement
LucidORG . People + AI.
ECI measures both human teams and human-AI interaction. One instrument scores your people, your agents, and the handoffs between them.
02 . Verification
Lie detectors.
Every AI output passes through fact-check before it ships. If the system can't cite the source, it can't ship the claim.
03 . Architecture
Split agents.
Producer agents and verifier agents are separate. Built-in adversarial review on every action.
04 . Recovery
Full state with backups.
Append-only audit trail. Roll back any action. Reconstruct any decision. Replay any conversation.
05 . Routing
Systematic protection.
Rate limits. Budget caps. Scope guards. Every request routed through governance. Your CFO will sleep better.
06 . Override
Kill switches.
Emergency shutoff per system, agent, task. One operator can stop every running pod across the stack in one keystroke.
